Creator Research Desk

Creator Research Desk is a private desktop tool used by a single creator to manage his own YouTube channels.

It runs only on the owner's Windows computer. It uses YouTube API Services to:

  • read analytics for the owner's own channels;
  • add translated titles and descriptions to the owner's own videos;
  • update titles and thumbnails of the owner's own videos after his approval.

It is not offered to the public and does not access other people's channels.
