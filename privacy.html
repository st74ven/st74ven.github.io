Privacy Policy and Terms of Use – Creator Research Desk

Last updated: November 19, 2026

What the app is

Creator Research Desk is a private desktop application that runs only on its owner's computer. It is used only by its owner to manage, upload and schedule videos on his own YouTube channels. It is not distributed to other users and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

With the account holder's permission, the app:

  • reads channel and video information and YouTube Analytics reports for the authorized channels;
  • uploads the owner's own finished videos to the owner's own channels as private videos with a scheduled publish time, and sets their title, description, tags, category, language, "made for kids" setting and altered-content setting;
  • sets the custom thumbnail, adds the video to one of the channel's own playlists and, after the video is public, posts the owner's own first comment once;
  • can update titles, descriptions, translations and thumbnails of those channels' own videos.

The app does not access, collect or change data of other people or other channels.

Storage and sharing

All data is stored only on the owner's computer. Login tokens are kept in the Windows Credential Manager. For each uploaded video the app keeps the video ID, title, scheduled time and status, only so that a video is never uploaded twice and the first comment is posted once. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app and this page use no cookies or tracking. Title and description text may be sent to an AI service solely to write or translate the owner's own video texts.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Signing out in the app deletes the stored token. Locally stored data can be deleted by removing the app's folder. Data received from YouTube is not kept longer than needed for the app to work.

Terms of use

This app is a personal tool and is not provided to anyone else. It is used in line with the YouTube Terms of Service and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
